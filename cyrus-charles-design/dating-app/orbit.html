Imagine a dating app made by Meta. It starts with eligible people in your extended friend circle, using your Instagram, Facebook, and contact connections, along with what it already knows about you.

You set your type: how close they live, their age, education and career, plus religion, everyday habits, and family plans. As you adjust your preferences, the app shows exactly how many people in your starting pool still match.

When you’re ready, the app introduces five matching people. Their photos appear shuffled, with individual trait details hidden. Choose up to two to invite on a date. You make your choices once a week: one date if one accepts, two if both do.
