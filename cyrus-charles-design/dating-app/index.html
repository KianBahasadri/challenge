This dating app connects you with people in your friend circle. It scans your Instagram, contacts, and mutual Meta networks to source a pool of eligible matches rather than strangers.

Instead of swiping, you specify your type using custom preferences such as minimum height, minimum income, and specific traits or credentials—like wanting someone who works out regularly or someone who holds a PhD.

Based on your specifications, the app shows in real time exactly how many eligible people in your social circle qualify, dynamically growing and shrinking your candidate pool as you adjust your criteria.

You review the qualifying candidates and select five people based purely on their character traits and lifestyle profile.

Once you choose your five, the app reveals what those five people look like, but decouples the traits from the faces. You see the five faces and the five trait profiles shuffled, so you know they match up to the group, but you do not know who is who.

From those five, you select two people you are interested in going on a date with.

You can run this process once per week. You go on one date per week, or up to two dates if both people accept. There is a maximum limit of two dates per week.

There is no swiping and no chatting with multiple people at once.
